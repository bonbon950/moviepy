# Bjorn's Shorts Rulebook

The rulebook for every short I edit for Bjorn. **Every rule here comes from Bjorn's own instructions,**
his 14 sample edits, his 3 reference videos, and his notes on the test edits. Version 1.1.
When a rule and a habit disagree, the rule wins. Tested on: *"A POWER RANGER BROKE KAI'S NAIL 😭"*.

---

## 0. The goal
**Entertaining, scroll-stopping shorts that people watch to the end.** Every choice is about
retention: hook in the first seconds, no boring frames, payoff at the end.

Creators: **Kai Cenat, TJR, Duke Dennis, Rakai, Fanum** (and their crews).

---

## 1. Hard rules — never break

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Format | **9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps, always 1080p** |
| Output per material | **6–8 SOLID videos every time** Bjorn sends a link or material |
| Length | **40–50 seconds** |
| Header | **Always** a header at the top, visible the whole video |
| Captions | **Always moving** — never static |
| Action tags | **Every laugh** gets a tag + SFX; other actions too |
| SFX | Heavy, **especially the first 3–5 s**, and **always matched to the scene** |
| Music | Quiet background bed, **viral/commonly used sounds only**, matched to the scene |
| Crop | **Minimal** — never crop so much it disturbs the viewer |
| Twitch chat / overlays | **Keep visible** |
| Export | ≥ 10 Mbps H.264, AAC 192k, loud and consistent (target −10 LUFS, −1 dBTP) |
| Delivery | Upload every video to Bjorn's Google Drive folder **`OUTPUT (<date>)`** |

**Never:** CapCut end card or watermark · garbled or wrong captions · uncensored curse words in
captions · a header that lies about the clip · nonsense SFX that don't fit · new/unknown music ·
low-res or stretched emoji · dead air.

---

## 2. ⭐ Finding the clips (MOST IMPORTANT RULE)
**Every link or material → 6–8 SOLID videos.** All of them must follow every rule in this book.
Finding the right moments matters more than any effect.

1. Bjorn sends a **Twitch VOD/clip link** → download directly. (YouTube blocks cloud downloads →
   Bjorn puts YouTube videos in Google Drive and sends the link.)
2. **Scan the whole stream**, second by second: laughter, screaming, crowd, loudness. Skip
   music-only stretches and the parts Twitch muted.
3. **Transcribe the whole stream** and read every top spike; look at the frames.
4. A moment is **SOLID** only if it has:
   - a **complete mini-story**: setup → chaos/reaction → payoff, understandable with no context;
   - **real entertainment**: big laugh, roast, shock, prank, fail, argument, wholesome/hype peak;
   - a **hook in the first 3 s** (a line or reaction strong enough for the cold open);
   - enough material for **40–50 s** without filler;
   - clean, usable audio (not muted by Twitch, not buried under music).
5. Pick **6–8 different moments**: no overlapping footage, no repeating the same joke. Prefer
   moments other clippers haven't already posted.
6. Rank them by entertainment; edit all of them; run the checklist (section 11) on every one.
7. **Never pad with weak clips.** If a stream truly has fewer than 6 solid moments, say so
   clearly, tell Bjorn how many it has and why, and deliver only the solid ones.
8. Download only the chosen moments in 1080p60.

## 3. Structure (40–50 s)
- **Cold open (0–2 s):** start on the peak — the loudest/funniest line — then rewind with a
  record scratch + `*MOMENTS EARLIER*`.
- **Story:** jump cuts remove every pause; keep only what moves the story.
- **Payoff:** end right after the punchline, on a final hit (SFX + emoji + zoom).

---

## 4. Header
- **Job:** stop the scroll and make them watch to the end. A bold claim, an open loop or tension —
  **never a plain label.**
  - ❌ "KAI RECEIVED" → ✅ "IShowSpeed Sent Kai Something He Didn't Expect 😭"
  - ❌ "KAI'S IPHONE DUO" → ✅ "Kai Found A Phone Apple Hasn't Released Yet"
  - ✅ "A POWER RANGER BROKE KAI'S NAIL 😭"
- Must be **true** — the video has to pay it off.
- 1–2 lines, ~8 words max, Anton font, ALL CAPS, thick black outline + shadow.
- **Color: whatever pops hardest against that clip's footage** (contrast first; any color, not
  just yellow/green; can change every video).
- 0–1 emoji at the end, **high-res 3D**. Slams in at the start (scale bounce) then stays.
- Sits in the top blur band.

---

## 5. Captions
- **One word at a time**, pop animation (65% → 115% → 100%), Anton, ALL CAPS, black outline.
- **Color per speaker** — each person keeps one color all video (e.g. Kai white, Agent light blue).
  Phone/voicemail/TTS voices get their own color.
- **Money and numbers in green** (`$300`).
- **Censor curse words** in captions: F\*CK, SH\*T, B\*TCH, N\*GGA…
- Hand-check every word against the audio; fix speech-to-text mistakes. Never auto-caption
  foreign-language speech.
- Placement: lower part of the video band, center.
- Other caption styles from Bjorn's catalog (2–4 word highlight box, keyword color, bounce)
  may be used when they fit the clip better — **one style per video**.

---

## 6. ⭐ Action tags
Every time something happens that isn't speech, show it as a tag between asterisks **with a
matching sound effect**.
- **Laughs are mandatory:** `*KAI LAUGHS*`, `*EVERYONE LAUGHS*`… (name the person only when sure).
  Found automatically with the audio tagger, checked by eye. Pair with a pink flash.
- Others: `*NAIL BREAKS*`, `*KAI SCREAMS*`, `*SEARCHING FOR HIS NAIL*`, `*NO SORRY*`,
  `*CALLS SPEED*`, `*CONFUSED*`, `*HYPED*`…
- Look: Anton, ALL CAPS, white with thick black outline; variant **white on a dark red box**.
- **Position: never all in one spot.**
  - At **eye level** inside the video, left / center / right, next to whatever it describes.
  - **Sometimes** in the empty bottom blur band (the area Bjorn circled). Mix it up.
- **Size varies** per tag (≈ 90–145 px) — noticeably bigger on big moments, never covering the screen.
- Slight tilt (±2–7°), pop in with overshoot, slow grow while on screen. Hold for the action (0.5–2 s).
- Must match the footage on screen at that moment.

---

## 7. Emoji stickers
- On memorable moments (laughs, shock, cringe, roasts) — **sometimes, not on every beat.**
- **Always matched to the scene:** something weird/unbelievable → 💀💀💀 · crying/loss → 😭😭 ·
  laughing → 😂😂 · rage → 🤬 · searching → 👀 · wisdom/facts → 💯 · respect → 🫡 ·
  money → 💰💸 · hype → 🔥 · beauty/nails → 💅💔.
- Usually in the empty bottom band, sometimes a corner at eye level. 1–3 emoji, slightly tilted,
  pop in, gentle bob.
- **Quality:** high-res glossy 3D emoji (256 px, iPhone-style look) — never small or pixelated.

---

## 8. Sound effects
- **Heavy in the first 3–5 s** — stack 2–4 hits so viewers stop scrolling: impact on the first
  frame/header slam, a hit on the first big word, rewind scratch, whoosh.
- After the hook: an SFX on **every real beat** (reaction, roast, reveal, punchline, action tag).
- **Every SFX must fit the scene.** Ask: does it make the moment hit harder or funnier? If it's
  just noise, cut it. No nonsense sounds.
- **Bass boom / beat drop on the punchline**, synced with the tag + flash + zoom.
- Cut the music to silence for 0.3–0.5 s right before a big hit, then slam back in.
- SFX never bury the dialogue.
- Library: `sfx_manifest.tsv` (67 viral meme sounds: vine boom, bass boom/drop, 808, FAHHH, bruh,
  oh hell nah, emotional damage, laughs, record scratch, whoosh, siren, cash register, airhorn…)
  + Bjorn's own pack (gunshot, NANI?!, bell, DING…).
  - Roast lands → gunshot / vine boom · Shock/reveal → dramatic hit / bass boom · Confusion →
    NANI?! / huh · Fail → bruh / sad trombone · Money → cash register · Laugh → laugh SFX.

---

## 9. Background music
- A **quiet** bed under the clip (≈ 17–21 dB under the dialogue, dips further while people talk).
- **Viral, commonly used shorts sounds only** — familiar sounds keep retention. No new/unknown tracks.
- **Must match the scene.** If nothing fits, no music rather than the wrong music.
- **The bed changes with the mood inside one clip** (e.g. Oh No No No while things go wrong →
  sad violin for the "that's just life" part → Curb Your Enthusiasm for the ending).
- Library: `beds_manifest.tsv` — sneaky/comedic (Sneaky Snitch, Fluffing a Duck, Pink Panther,
  Mission Impossible flute), investigation (Investigations, SpongeBob cop music), awkward
  (Jeopardy, elevator, Curb Your Enthusiasm), sad-funny (sad violin, Oh No No No), flex
  (Chopin Nocturne) · `beats_manifest.tsv` — Brazilian phonk / montagem for hype.
- Bjorn will send more track names → add them here.
- Tip for TikTok/IG: also add a trending sound in-app at upload for reach.

---

## 10. Visuals & motion
- **Layout:** blur sandwich — 16:9 footage full-width in the middle, blurred zoomed copy fills
  top and bottom. Minimal crop only.
- **Zooms — noticeable but never shaky:**
  - Punch zoom (115–125%, snaps in 2–3 frames) on reactions, roasts, SFX hits.
  - Slow push (100 → 108% over several seconds) on talking stretches.
- **Color flashes** (2–4 frames) on hits: red on impacts, pink on laughs, white on shouts,
  black & white on awkward/no-response moments.
- Punchy grade: a bit more saturation and contrast.
- Optional: meme/B-roll cutaway (1–2 s visual joke matching the line), whip transitions.

---

## 11. Before delivering — checklist
- [ ] 1080×1920, 30 fps, 40–50 s, ≥ 10 Mbps
- [ ] Header true, intriguing, readable, nothing cut off
- [ ] Every laugh tagged + SFX; tags match what's on screen; positions and sizes vary
- [ ] Captions correct, censored, speaker colors right, no overlapping words
- [ ] Emoji crisp and matched to the scene
- [ ] Hook: 2–4 SFX in the first 3–5 s; punchline hits land
- [ ] Music: viral, quiet, matches each part; no dialogue buried
- [ ] No CapCut watermark, no dead air
- [ ] Review frames at every tag, sticker and hit before sending

## 12. Delivery
- Create (or reuse) a Google Drive folder in Bjorn's account named **`OUTPUT (<date>)`**,
  e.g. `OUTPUT (November 6, 2026)` — the date the videos are made, in **Philippine time (Asia/Manila)**.
  Same day → same folder. Uploader verified working (68 MB clip in ~2.5 min).
- Upload all 6–8 videos there. File names: **`CLIP1.mp4`, `CLIP2.mp4`, …** in ranking order (Bjorn's request:
  easy to identify). The same CLIP numbers are used in the POSTING PLAN sheet.
- Also send a short summary in chat: folder link, and for each video the header, VOD timestamp
  and why the moment is strong.
- **CHECK before, DOUBLE-CHECK after — every Drive change.** Bjorn wants a clean, organized Drive.
  - Before uploading: list the OUTPUT folder; never upload a file whose name is already there
    (the uploader skips it automatically) and never create a second folder for the same date.
  - After uploading: list the folder again and confirm every file is there, with the right size,
    and that there are no extras.
  - Restoring from trash (helper v2 `restore`): only files from OUTPUT folders, and only when
    Bjorn asks or after a mistake of mine; list the folder afterwards to confirm.
- **Never delete or trash anything in Bjorn's Drive on my own.** Only ask to trash an item if it is
  a true **duplicate** or **nonsense/junk** (e.g. a broken partial upload), and say exactly which file
  and why. Improved versions are uploaded *next to* the originals only if Bjorn asks — never replace.
- Upload path: the Apps Script uploader Bjorn installs once (`tools/drive_uploader.gs`) +
  `tools/upload_drive.py`. The Drive connector alone can create the folder but cannot upload files
  this big.

## 13. Posting to YouTube Shorts
- **Nothing is posted without Bjorn's approval.** Flow: batch → Drive `OUTPUT (date)` → rows added to the
  Google Sheet **POSTING PLAN** (one sheet, kept simple) → Bjorn watches and types **YES** → it gets posted.
- Channel: Bjorn's YouTube on blovesarmiento@gmail.com.
- **Caption (YouTube title):** edgy, provocative, but always true to the clip; built to trigger comments
  (a take people will argue with, a question, a "be honest…"). Under 100 characters, 1 emoji.
  e.g. "Y'all would switch up too if you had his money. Stop acting humble 😁"
- **Description:** hook line + a direct question/CTA ("Who was wrong? 👇", "Tag that friend") + hashtags.
- **Hashtags (US audience):** creator + crew (#kaicenat #amp #rakai…) + topic (#halloween #nails…) +
  reach (#kaicenatclips #twitchclips #ampclips #shorts #fyp). 8–12 total.
- **Timing:** one video per US active window, never all at once. Slots (US Eastern → Philippines, while
  ET = PHT − 13 h): 8:00 AM→9:00 PM, 12:30 PM⭐→1:30 AM, 3:00 PM→4:00 AM, 5:30 PM→6:30 AM,
  **7:00 PM⭐⭐→8:00 AM (best clip)**, 9:00 PM⭐→10:00 AM, 10:30 PM→11:30 AM. Refine with channel analytics.
- Bjorn schedules each approved clip himself in the YouTube app using the sheet (copy caption/description,
  Visibility → Schedule at POST TIME 🇵🇭), then types SCHEDULED.
- **ONE POSTING PLAN sheet forever — never create another.** New batches are appended as rows above HOW TO USE.
- **Keep it clean (Bjorn's rule):** before adding a new batch (and whenever asked) run `tools/plan_cleanup.py`:
  delete rows where APPROVE = YES and the POST TIME has passed or STATUS says POSTED (already live), and rows where APPROVE = NO
  (rejected). Blank APPROVE = not reviewed → never deleted. Dry run first, then `--apply`, then read back.
  Only sheet rows are deleted — never the videos in Drive.

## Appendix A — What Bjorn's 14 sample edits showed (scan)

## Appendix B — What the 3 reference videos do (Bjorn's ideal style)
- Action tags (`*CALLS SPEED*`, `*KAI LAUGHS*`…), one-word captions with speaker colors,
  fast cuts (0.5–3 s), punch zooms, color flashes, whip transitions, meme cutaways,
  bass drops on punchlines, background music bed throughout.
- Differences kept on purpose: references have no header (we always do), are 22–28 s (we do
  40–50 s), and crop tight (we crop minimally).
